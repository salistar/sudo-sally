Features

Built for people who love Sudoku

Every detail of SallySudo is designed to make solving puzzles feel smooth, rewarding and genuinely fun. Here's everything inside.

🧩

Five difficulty levels

Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Diabolique. Each tier ramps up the challenge, with level progression and unlocking so you always have a goal to chase.

🧠

Daily brain training

A fresh puzzle every day to keep your mind sharp. Build a streak and make Sudoku part of your daily routine — train your brain daily.

💡

Smart hints

When you're stuck, a smart hint fills in the correct number for the selected cell — no guessing, no broken puzzles, just a gentle nudge forward.

🔍

Auto-solve & reveal

Want to see how it ends? Reveal the full solution instantly with auto-solve, perfect for learning new solving techniques.

✏️

Pencil notes

Mark candidate numbers in any cell to track your possibilities, exactly like jotting notes when solving on paper.

⚔️

Challenge & 1v1 mode

Play head-to-head against a friend with a live side-by-side opponent board. Join via quick matchmaking or share a code to invite anyone.

📊

Detailed stats

See puzzles solved, best times, win rates and streaks. Watch your progress grow with clear, beautiful charts.

🏅

Achievements

Unlock badges as you hit milestones — first Diabolique solve, a 10-game win streak, a flawless puzzle and many more.

📈

ELO ranking

Every challenge match adjusts your ELO rating. Climb the competitive ladder and prove you're the sharpest solver around.

🌍

Multi-language & RTL

Fully translated into English, French and Arabic — including complete right-to-left layout support that feels native, not bolted on.

🔐

Google sign-in or Guest

Sign in with Google to sync your progress and join ranked play, or jump straight in as a Guest with progress saved on your device.

✨

Gorgeous animated UI

A polished dark interface with soft glows, smooth transitions and satisfying animations on every tap, move and win.
