Installing on Android

Four quick steps

Installing an APK takes less than a minute.

1

Download the APK

Tap the green “Download for Android (APK)” button above. The sudoku-sally.apk file will save to your Downloads.

2

Allow unknown sources

When prompted, allow your browser or Files app to “Install unknown apps.” You can turn this off again afterwards — it just lets Android install an app from outside the Play Store.

3

Open the APK

Tap the downloaded file (in Downloads or your notification shade) to launch the Android installer.

4

Install & play

Press “Install,” wait a moment, then “Open.” Sign in with Google or play as a Guest and you're ready to go!
